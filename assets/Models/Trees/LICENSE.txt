Tree models in this folder are derived from Poly Haven assets released under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://polyhaven.com/license).

island_tree_02/  derived from "Island Tree 02"  https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_02
                 authors: Rob Tuytel (scanning, processing), Rico Cilliers (cleanup, processing)
jacaranda_tree/  derived from "Jacaranda Tree"  https://polyhaven.com/a/jacaranda_tree
                 authors: Rico Cilliers (all), Rob Tuytel (guidance)

Modifications (tools/tree_reduce.py, Project July 2024, 2026-10-25): each scanned leaf mesh was
replaced by one textured quad, twig and trunk meshes were decimated by vertex clustering, three
levels of detail were written per species variant, and a fraction of leaf quads carry a vertex-colour
tint used as a stand-in for blossom. Only the 1k diffuse and normal textures are kept. The
"krishnachura", "radhachura" and "shade" variants are the same island tree with different tints;
"jarul" is the jacaranda. None of these is a botanically correct model of the named species.
