## Addendum 5, same day: tree models

### What was checked

No game-ready, CC0 or CC-BY, realistic tree model of krishnachura, jarul, radhachura or banyan was found (Poly Haven, ambientCG, Kenney, Quaternius, Sketchfab's downloadable CC0 filter, OpenGameArt were searched). Poly Haven publishes CC0 photogrammetry trees, but as 1.07 M (island_tree_02) and 3.86 M (jacaranda_tree) triangle scans with one small mesh per leaf, which no laptop GPU can draw thirty times.

### Completed and verified

- `tools/tree_reduce.py` (numpy only; the `pip install` of a decimation library was refused by the sandbox) turns each scan into three game levels of detail: every leaf mesh becomes one textured quad (principal-component plane, affine UV fit), leaves are subsampled and enlarged per level, twigs and trunk are decimated by vertex clustering, the branch texture transform is baked into the UVs, and a fraction of leaves carries a vertex-colour blossom tint. Output: island 94 k / 31 k / 6 k and jacaranda 150 k / 40 k / 8 k triangles, 39 MB of assets under `assets/Models/Trees/` with `LICENSE.txt`; registered in `docs/ASSET_REGISTER.md`.
- `world/TreeLibrary.java` loads each variant once, converts the glTF PBR materials to `Lighting.j3md` (diffuse + normal map, alpha discard 0.5 and double-sided for leaves, vertex-colour tint), and places clones with a `TreeLodControl` that switches levels at 45 m and 130 m from the camera. `CampusWorld.buildVegetation` and `buildBanyanTree` place 31 trees through it, keep trunk colliders, and fall back to the old sphere tree only if a model fails to load (log line: `31 model trees (0 procedural fallbacks)`).
- Build: `gradlew :game:compileJava :game:test` passes (9/9 tests, two new: LOD band selection and presence of all 12 model files).
- Automated 300-frame runs on this Windows machine (1280×720, integrated GPU; not the M4 target):

| View | Steady-state frame time (last 100 frames) |
|---|---|
| spawn (before this change, same day) | 26.9–27.6 ms |
| spawn, trees | 33.4 ms |
| TSC view | 30.8 ms |
| west road, close view of krishnachura | 32.6 ms |
| banyan view | 32.3 ms |
| library view (six trees near) | 40.1 ms |

- Screenshots inspected: `docs/screenshots/trees_2026-10-25_{spawn,close,library,banyan,tsc}_v1.png`. The trees read as real trees: textured bark, leaning scanned trunks, leaf clusters with light and shadow, scarlet and mauve blossom patches. The sphere canopies are gone from every view.

### Honest limits

- Species: the island tree is an unidentified 3.4 m broadleaf scan scaled 2.6–4.4×, so its leaves and bark are too large for a krishnachura (which has fine bipinnate foliage) and it has no banyan aerial roots; the jacaranda has feathery leaves like a jarul only at a distance. Blossom is a tint on ordinary leaf quads, not flower geometry. All of this is recorded in `docs/REFERENCE_LEDGER.md` section 6 and the asset register.
- Cost: the trees add roughly 5–13 ms per frame on this integrated GPU in the views measured, most of it the alpha-tested leaf quads of the LOD-0 trees within 45 m. The M4 target remains unmeasured. If it is too slow there, the first levers are a shorter LOD-0 distance and a lower LOD-0 leaf fraction in `tools/tree_reduce.py`.
- The asset folder is 39 MB because the three island-tree variants duplicate the trunk and twig geometry; a shared-geometry variant was not done.
- Capture runs: three automated runs ended before frame 300 without the profile summary and without an exception in the log (one library run stopped at frame 240, two close-view runs at frames 45 and 165; no quit-menu line was logged, so a keystroke or window close reaching the test window is the most likely explanation). They were repeated and the repeats completed; the cause was not established.

## Addendum 7, 2026-10-26: reversed movement, mouse look, slogans

The owner reported that the up arrow moved Arif towards the screen.

### Completed and verified

- Cause: `PlayerControl.update` rotated the input vector by the camera yaw with a formula that yields the opposite of the camera's forward and right vectors (the orbit camera with yaw θ sits at (sin θ, −cos θ) from the player and looks along (−sin θ, cos θ); the old code produced (sin θ, −cos θ) for "forward"). Both axes were therefore mirrored. Fixed by building the direction from the camera forward and right vectors explicitly.
- Mouse look had the same mirror on yaw: mouse-left increased the yaw, which swings the camera to the player's left and turns the view right. Sign flipped in `ProjectJulyApp.onAnalog`; pitch was already correct.
- New test hook `TEST_HOLD=Forward|Backward|Left|Right|CamLeft|CamRight` holds an input from frame 30 to 150 of an automated run and logs the player and camera positions. Results (spawn at (0, 17), camera south of the player):
