## Addendum, same day: character milestone

**Scope:** replace the procedural box character with a properly licensed rigged humanoid, verified in the running game.

### Completed and verified

- **Asset sourced and licensed.** Quaternius *Universal Animation Library* (Standard tier), CC0 1.0 as stated on the author's itch.io page; glTF obtained from the `J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library` mirror. Files, sizes, URLs and licence recorded in `docs/ASSET_REGISTER.md`; `LICENSE` committed next to the model.
- **Integration.** `PlayerMesh` now loads the glTF, finds the `AnimComposer` and `SkinningControl`, swaps the PBR materials for the scene's Phong materials tinted per character, scales the model to 1.72 m with feet at y = 0, and selects `Idle_Loop` / `Walk_Loop` / `Jog_Fwd_Loop` / `Sprint_Loop` / `Crouch_Idle_Loop` / `Crouch_Fwd_Loop` / `Jump_Loop` from the controller state, with clip speed scaled to movement speed. Public API unchanged, so `CompanionNPC` and `ProjectJulyApp` needed no structural change. `PlayerControl` passes the airborne flag. `assets/` is now registered with the asset manager via `FileLocator`.
- **Spawn facing.** Arif now spawns facing the Raju sculpture (heading 180°) instead of the camera.
- **Log hygiene.** The glTF loader's 3,405 "linear interpolation" warnings are silenced during the character load only; the run log dropped from ~3,500 lines to 106.
- **Evidence.** Build and 7/7 tests pass. Two 300-frame runs completed. `character_2026-10-25_spawn_v1.png` shows Arif and Rafi as mannequins in idle; `character_2026-10-25_spawn_v2.png` shows the jog clip mid-stride. The profile line prints `Character: model loaded=true, clip=Jog_Fwd_Loop, clip time=0.30 s`.

### Honest limits

- The mannequin has **no face, hair or clothing**. It satisfies "licensed, human proportions, suitable animation" and no longer fails the gate on primitive geometry, but it does not yet look like a student in shirt and jeans. A dressed model on the same rig is the next character task.
- During the second automated run, mouse and keyboard input reached the game window (camera yaw 280°, character displaced 8 m). This was not scripted. It happens to demonstrate locomotion, but the spawn-facing change is therefore verified only by code reading, not by that screenshot.
- Clip nominal speeds used to reduce foot sliding are estimates by eye, not published values.
- Talking idle during dialogue is wired in `PlayerMesh.setTalking` but not yet called from the dialogue flow.
- Static-run frame time on this Windows machine with four skinned characters: 21–26 ms average over 300 frames at 1280×720. Not a benchmark; not the target Mac.

## Addendum 3, same day: reference-based campus

**Scope:** make the Level 01 campus recognizable: rebuild the Raju sculpture, the TSC and the Central Library from documented references, give them real materials, keep collisions working, show the result in screenshots with an honest note of what is approximate.

### What was checked

- OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) for the footprints and positions of every landmark around TSC Mor; queries and raw responses committed under `docs/reference/osm/`. Game coordinates are metres from the sculpture, +X east, +Z south, at true scale (TSC union block 67 m south, Library Building II 63–121 m north-west).
- Daily Star feature on the TSC programme (auditorium 170 × 85 ft under a parabolic vault; union building 3 storeys, 158 × 37 ft, RC frame, brick, double roof, jali, covered walkways; cafeteria of two wings), Wikipedia (Raju composition and history; TSC roof and courts), Banglapedia (library: two blocks on pilotis, ramp, vaulted third block), UNESCO tentative list (library dates). archnet returned HTTP 429 and was not used.
- Each claim is listed in `docs/REFERENCE_LEDGER.md` as verified, supported-but-incomplete, or unverified/chosen. Conflicts recorded: TSC union block 3 storeys (Daily Star) vs `building:levels=5` (OSM); auditorium width 25.9 m vs 21.2 m; TSC dates 1961 vs 1962.

### Completed and verified

- `world/CampusWorld.java` rewritten (true-scale layout, textured materials with world-space UV projection and tangents, custom parabolic-vault and disc meshes, yawed TSC frame with grid colliders, walkable-surface heights, per-material static batching: 853 geometries -> 22 batches, 408 colliders).
- `character/PlayerControl.java`: ground-height sampler so kerbs, the island and plazas are walked on rather than through.
- `ProjectJulyApp.java`: spawn moved to the ring road, companions and objectives moved to the new positions (Tanvir in front of the library, Nusrat on the TSC plaza); `TEST_SPAWN`, `TEST_CAM_PITCH`, `TEST_SHOT` environment overrides for verification captures; steady-state frame time (last 100 frames) added to the profile; camera yaw sign corrected for spawn headings.
- `ui/ProjectJulyHUD.java`: compass bearing turned by 180° so the display matches the layout (facing the sculpture from spawn now reads N).
- Six ambientCG CC0 texture sets registered in `docs/ASSET_REGISTER.md` and `assets/Textures/LICENSE.txt`.
- Build and 7/7 tests pass. Five automated 300-frame runs, exit 0 each. Screenshots inspected: `campus_2026-10-25_spawn_v3.png`, `_tsc_v2.png`, `_tsc_auditorium_v1.png`, `_library_v2.png`. Earlier captures (`_v1`, `spawn_v2`) showed red-stained asphalt (Asphalt012), an inverted compass and the wrong camera yaw for non-default headings; all three were fixed and the captures replaced.

### Honest limits

- Footprints and positions are referenced; **elevations are not**: storey heights above the OSM `height=15` tag, window patterns, the clerestory under the TSC roof, the library fins, the vault crown height (14 m) and the sculpture's figure size, plinth and material are modelling choices and are labelled so in the ledger.
- Buildings are still primitives and two custom meshes; no photographic reference was matched, so "recognizable" means massing and arrangement, not likeness.
- Only the roundabout footprint and the Shahid Minar Sarak bearing are from OSM; the other three road arms are approximate.
- The library ramp cannot be walked up (no slope support in `PlayerControl`); it is blocked by colliders.
- Vegetation is unchanged (sphere canopies), only repositioned.
- Performance on this Windows machine (AMD Radeon integrated graphics, 1280×720, shadows + SSAO + FXAA): average over 300 frames 30–45 ms including start-up; steady state over the last 100 frames 21.6–32.4 ms (31–46 FPS) depending on view. The clothed-character build measured 24–27 ms average on the same machine, so the larger world costs roughly 10 ms here. Not the target Mac; no 60 FPS claim.
- Not played by hand today: collisions and walkable heights were exercised only by the static runs (the character stood on the ring road and the island at the expected heights in the captures).
