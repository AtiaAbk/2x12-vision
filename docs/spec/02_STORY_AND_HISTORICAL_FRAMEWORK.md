# Project July 2024 — Story & Historical Framework

**This is a narrative direction, not a verified chronology. Research each real event before scripting it.**

## Premise
Arif, a fictional University of Dhaka second-year Political Science student, and his close friends experience the atmosphere, uncertainty, solidarity, and risks around Bangladesh's July–August 2024 student movement. Keep the human story personal while respecting the real historical context.

## Characters
- **Arif:** fictional ordinary student; motivated by fairness, student rights, solidarity, and helping friends survive.
- **Rafi, Tanvir, Nusrat:** close friends who trust one another. Their personal backgrounds, dialogue, family circumstances, and individual missions are fictional unless independently documented.
- Avoid implausible combat expertise, superhero behavior, or melodrama that undermines realism.

## Opening
Start on an ordinary campus morning. Arif meets his friends naturally; the player gains control, explores, and discovers optional conversations and locations. Establish everyday student life before the story's tension grows.

## High-level structure
1. Campus introduction and optional exploration.
2. Growing tension conveyed through researched context, dialogue, environmental storytelling, or media.
3. Grounded decisions about helping others, staying together, avoiding danger, and navigating changing circumstances.
4. Wider researched locations, including a possible Jahangirnagar University sequence and relevant Dhaka areas.
5. Later climax/ending concept around the Ganabhaban road area and Shaheed Minar.

This is not a finalized mission script. Do not invent historical specifics to fill gaps. The ending location and route are creative directions until researched and verified.

## Tone
Story and historical experience are the main priorities. Use connected mission areas, main and optional side missions, exploration, interaction, and grounded action. Favor evasion, defense, rescue, escape, survival, solidarity, and difficult choices over power fantasy. Do not use violence merely for spectacle.

## Historical research ledger
For every date, location, slogan, quotation, event, memorial, or claim, record:
- Exact claim and claim ID.
- Direct URL, title, publisher.
- Publication date and event date.
- What the source directly supports.
- Cross-check sources.
- Status: verified / supported-but-incomplete / disputed / unverified.
- Limitations and last-checked date.

Prefer primary records and credible reporting. Never invent a quote and attribute it to a real person. Do not fabricate dates, casualty figures, perpetrators, or precise routes. Mark dramatized/composite scenes clearly and preserve disagreements between sources.

## Deliverables
1. Short synopsis.
2. Fictional character profiles.
3. Mission outline with objectives and optional content.
4. Historical source ledger.
5. Fact-versus-fiction matrix per mission.
6. Unresolved claims requiring review.
7. Bangla/English subtitle and script document where needed.
