# Project July 2024 — Visual, Audio & Technical Quality

## Visual target
Realistic, detailed, cinematic third-person action-adventure set in Bangladesh during July–August. References such as Where Winds Meet, Black Myth, God of War, Cyberpunk, and Wildlands express broad quality aspirations only. Do not copy proprietary assets, UI, music, or specific creative expression.

## Environment and art direction
- Believable Bangladeshi summer/monsoon setting, lush natural vegetation, varied tree silhouettes, leaves, ground cover, and restrained wind animation.
- Balanced warm daylight, natural shadows, convincing material response, coherent color grading.
- Avoid washed-out gray, monochrome/winter looks, overly saturated colors, black background caused by rendering errors, cartoon proportions, and generic untextured cuboids.
- Keep scale consistent across characters, doors, stairs, roads, trees, and buildings.
- Level 01 should research and represent Curzon Hall surroundings, Central Library, Hakim Chattar, TSC, and the Anti-Terrorism Raju Memorial Sculpture.
- Do not label generic blocks as accurate replicas. Verify architectural claims and attributions with reliable references.

## Character and camera
- A milestone called realistic must not use a box body and sphere head as the final character.
- Use a properly licensed humanoid model, believable proportions, clothing/materials, and suitable animation.
- Arif should look like an ordinary student in casual shirt/trousers or jeans, with ID/backpack where appropriate.
- Third-person camera must have useful framing, smooth follow, sensible clipping, and no geometry penetration.
- Bengali text must be upright, readable, correctly shaped, and not mirrored.
- Remove debug overlays from polished screenshots unless demonstrating diagnostics.

## Visual acceptance gate
Check that the character is not primitive placeholder geometry; the campus is recognizable; lighting/background are correct; vegetation suits Bangladesh's summer/monsoon; materials/colors are coherent; Bengali text is legible; camera/collision work; and there are no missing textures or obvious rendering artifacts.
Use genuine screenshots from the running build. Concept art is not proof of an in-engine result.

## Audio
Create original instrumental music for a cinematic historical story in Bangladesh. The user likes broad emotional/cinematic qualities from games such as Where Winds Meet, but do not copy any specific track, melody, arrangement, or proprietary library. Create a distinct identity with restrained orchestral tension, emotional strings, atmospheric layers, and respectful regional instrumental colors.

Create original 1–2 second UI sounds: click, confirm, back/cancel, notification, objective update, warning. Keep the attack clear, decay pleasant, and peaks unclipped. Organize music, ambience, voice, UI, and gameplay effects separately. Document filename, purpose, duration, format, sample rate, channels, source/prompt, rights, and integration status. Bangla voice direction and Bangla/English subtitles are desired.

## Performance
Target MacBook Air M4, 16 GB RAM; aim for 1080p/60 FPS where feasible. Measure representative gameplay over sufficient duration and report resolution, settings, frame times/stability, memory use, and duration. An instantaneous FPS number is not proof of stable performance. Prefer measured, robust effects over expensive post-processing without visible benefit.

## Licensing
Record creator, URL, license, attribution, modification permission, commercial-use permission, restrictions, and integration status for every external asset. Publicly viewable does not mean reusable. Do not extract proprietary commercial game assets/audio. Document missing assets and label placeholders.

## Evidence
For each major visual milestone, launch the actual game, capture genuine screenshots (preferably 1920×1080 where supported), use another angle/clip if needed, compare with the prior build, and list improvements and limitations. Never fabricate screenshots, performance, or licenses.
